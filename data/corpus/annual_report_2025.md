# Kestrel Freight Analytics Annual Report 2025

> Fictional company created for LLM evaluation practice. All names and figures are invented.

Kestrel Freight Analytics, Inc. is headquartered in Omaha, Nebraska. The company was founded in 2017 and sells software and data products that help shipping and logistics companies plan freight routes more efficiently.

Leadership: Dana Whitfield serves as Chief Executive Officer (CEO). Marcus Oyelaran serves as Chief Financial Officer (CFO).

Financial results for fiscal year 2025: total revenue was $412.6 million, an increase of 18% from $349.7 million in fiscal year 2024. Net income for fiscal year 2025 was $38.9 million.

Revenue by segment: the Route Optimization Software segment contributed 62% of total revenue in 2025, and the Data Services segment contributed the remaining 38%.

Customers and employees: Kestrel served 2,850 enterprise customers at the end of 2025. The company had 1,240 full-time employees as of December 31, 2025.

Capital return: Kestrel does not pay a dividend. In November 2025 the board authorized a share buyback program of up to $25 million.

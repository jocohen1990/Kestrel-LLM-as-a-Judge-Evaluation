# Kestrel Freight Analytics Risk Factors 2026

> Fictional company created for LLM evaluation practice. All names and figures are invented.

Customer concentration: Kestrel's largest customer accounted for 9% of total revenue in 2025. Losing this customer would materially reduce revenue.

Cloud dependency: Kestrel runs its products on a single third-party cloud provider. An extended outage at that provider could disrupt service to customers.

Industry conditions: many Kestrel customers are trucking and logistics companies whose budgets are sensitive to fuel price volatility.

Debt and liquidity: Kestrel has a $150 million revolving credit facility. As of June 30, 2026, $40 million was drawn on the credit facility. Borrowings carry an interest rate of SOFR plus 1.75%.

Integration risk: the company may face difficulties integrating Bramblecote Telematics, which it acquired in June 2026.

Key personnel: Kestrel depends on its CEO, Dana Whitfield, and other senior leaders. The company does not maintain key-person life insurance.
